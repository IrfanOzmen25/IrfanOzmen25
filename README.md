📦 **Meijer mPerks** [Click Here For the Repo](https://github.com/IrfanOzmen25/Meijer_mPerks_Sample_Reward_Program):
1. Defined the Problem & Scope
- Designed a mock mPerks loyalty program for a grocery retailer.
- Set goals around improving personalization, forecasting demand, and measuring marketing campaign impact.


2. Data Collection & Preparation
- Gathered transactional, customer, product (SKU), and campaign data.
- Cleaned and merged datasets, handled missing values, and engineered features such as recency, frequency, and monetary value (RFM).


3. Recommendation System Development
- Built collaborative filtering models to recommend coupons based on customer–item interactions.
- Developed embedding-based recommenders to capture similarity between products and customers.
- Evaluated improvements in targeted coupon redemption rates using offline metrics (e.g., precision/recall).


4. Sales Forecasting Pipelines
- Implemented XGBoost models to predict weekly SKU-store sales using historical demand, promotions, and seasonality features.
- Built Prophet time series models for store- and item-level sales forecasting.
Compared models and achieved up to 70% higher accuracy over naïve baselines.


5. Personalized Campaign Generation with NLP
- Fine-tuned a GPT-2 model on historical marketing text and campaign messages.
- Used the fine-tuned model to generate personalized campaign messages tailored to customer segments and product interests.


6. Customer Segmentation Framework
- Applied K-Means clustering using RFM features and behavioral metrics to group customers into segments (e.g., high-value, at-risk, new).
- Used these segments to target recommendations, campaigns, and coupon offers more effectively.


7. Causal Inference & Campaign Lift Measurement
- Used uplift modeling and double machine learning (Double ML) to estimate the true incremental impact of campaigns vs. control groups.
- Distinguished correlation from causation to understand which campaigns actually drove additional sales and engagement.


8. Dashboarding & Visualization
- Built interactive dashboards to track:
- Model performance (forecast accuracy, recommendation metrics)
- Customer response and engagement
- Promotional ROI and campaign lift
- Enabled stakeholders to explore results and monitor system performance in real time.


9. Final Outcome
- Delivered an end-to-end, realistic mock loyalty platform demonstrating:
- Recommender systems
- Time-series forecasting
- NLP for personalization
- Customer segmentation
- Causal inference for marketing effectiveness
- Business-focused analytics and visualization.
